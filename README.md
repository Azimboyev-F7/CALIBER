# CALIBER 🎯
### *Know exactly where your college application stands.*

Most students apply to college hoping for the best. CALIBER gives you the honest, data-driven read that only private college counselors charging $5,000/hour usually provide — for free.

---

## What is CALIBER?

CALIBER is an AI-powered college application profile analyzer built for high school students who are serious about where they end up. It doesn't guess. It doesn't flatter. It scores your profile the way an admissions reader actually would — then tells you exactly what to do about it.

---

## Features 🚀

**📊 Profile Strength Score**
Get a precise Academic Rigor Score and Extracurricular Depth Score — built on a deterministic algorithm, not a vague AI guess. Understand exactly where you fall: Foundational / Solid / Strong / Standout.

**🏛️ Dream University Tracker**
Add your target schools and get an honest admission probability estimate based on your current profile scores. No sugarcoating — just the real picture so you can act on it.

**🧠 AI Counselor Chat**
An AI assistant that knows your profile deeply — your scores, your gaps, your strengths — and has a real conversation with you about what's holding you back and how to fix it.

**⚡ Gap Analysis**
Concrete, profile-specific flags: no leadership roles, no sustained 2+ year commitment, no measurable impact in activity descriptions. Only flags that are actually true for your profile.

**🎯 Extracurricular Recommendations**
Not generic advice. Personalized activity suggestions based on your current profile, intended major, and the gaps identified in your analysis.

**📝 Results Dashboard**
Visual breakdown of everything — scores, spike detection, AI-generated strengths, next steps checklist, and AI-rewritten activity descriptions ready for your Common App.

## Why CALIBER? 💡

Independent college counselors charge thousands of dollars to tell students what CALIBER tells them for free in under 60 seconds. The difference: CALIBER uses a transparent, explainable scoring engine — not a black box — so every score comes with a reason, and every recommendation comes with a next step.

---


## Scoring Methodology 📐

CALIBER's core engine is a **deterministic TypeScript function** — not an AI call. Academic Rigor and Extracurricular Depth scores are computed from real inputs (GPA, course rigor, hours per week, leadership, awards, sustained commitment) using a tiered activity model. AI is layered on top for narrative feedback only — because something this consequential deserves a score you can actually explain.
